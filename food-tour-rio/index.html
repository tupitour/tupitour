What the food tour is

Forget the tourist menus. This private food tour takes you through local markets, street fairs, hole-in-the-wall bars and the legendary botequins of Rio de Janeiro. Every stop is chosen by your guide for quality, authenticity and local soul.

Depending on the day and the market, the stops include petiscos like bolinho de bacalhau, pastel, acarajé, coxinha and pão de queijo, and full plates like feijoada, moqueca and fresh grilled fish. Tell us your restrictions or cravings and we build the food journey around you.

What is included

  • Certified local guide

What is not included

Food and drinks are paid directly at each stop, and all of them accept credit cards. Souvenirs are not included.
