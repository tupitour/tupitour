A private day in Rio between ship calls

A cruise day in Rio is short. A private tour lets you spend it on what you actually want to see, at your own pace, with a local guide who knows the city.

Send us your ship, date, the time you are on shore, the official all-aboard time and the number of people. We plan the route around those times and reply with a proposal.

What you can do

  • Christ the Redeemer and Sugarloaf Mountain, the classic Rio day
  • Santa Teresa, Selarón Steps, Lapa and historic downtown
  • Little Africa (Pequena África)
  • A mix of these, or something else you have in mind

What the quote covers

Your proposal confirms the route, the pick-up arrangement, the price and what is included, based on your ship's schedule and your group. It is a custom request, so these details are agreed with you before anything is booked.

Related tour

Our regular Private City Tour in Rio is USD 280 per group of up to 4 people for 6 hours, with a certified local guide and private transportation. Attraction tickets are not included in that tour.

How to ask

Message us on WhatsApp or the contact form on the home page with: ship name, date, time you are on shore, all-aboard time, and number of people.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a private tour on my cruise day in Rio?

Yes. Send us your ship, date, time on shore, all-aboard time and group size, and we reply with a proposal.

How much does a private shore excursion cost?

It depends on your schedule and group. For reference, our regular private City Tour Rio is USD 280 per group of up to 4 people for 6 hours. Ask us for a quote for your day.

Do you pick up at the cruise port?

Tell us your ship and arrival details when you ask for a quote, and we confirm the pick-up arrangement in the proposal.

Are attraction tickets included?

In our regular City Tour Rio, attraction tickets such as Christ the Redeemer and Sugarloaf are not included. We confirm what applies to your day in the quote.

What information do you need from me?

Ship name, date, time you are on shore, the official all-aboard time and the number of people.
